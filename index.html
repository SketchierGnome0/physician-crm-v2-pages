Fictional information only. Do not enter real patient information, protected health information (PHI), clinical details or confidential production records. This beta is not a patient-care application.

Stored on your device

CRM drafts and saved visits use an account-scoped encrypted local database. A local save is separate from a server acknowledgment.

Optional cloud testing

Supabase login and backup require a provisioned test identity and explicit workspace binding. Automatic backup starts Off; enabling it gives ongoing consent for eligible work finalized while On. Earlier work and work finalized while Off need separate manual review and consent. Backups include finalized visits and completed follow-up outcomes; drafts stay local. Uploads run while the app is active and may pause in the iOS background. No automatic team synchronization, guaranteed immediate recovery or real-user enrollment.

TestFlight is separate

Apple collects beta usage and crash diagnostics. Feedback and support messages can contain information you choose to share.
