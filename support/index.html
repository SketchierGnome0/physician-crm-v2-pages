Support & testing

A small, synthetic prototype for checking native CRM workflows and local durability.

Contact support

support@bracketwest.com

Operated by Igor Rodriguez. Include your app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, the fixed error label if shown, and short steps to reproduce. Use fictional examples only.

Preserve the existing installation. Do not uninstall the app, clear its storage, delete keys or try a recovery/reset procedure. Stop if an operation asks you to do so.

Update through TestFlight

  1. Open Apple’s TestFlight app with the account that accepted your existing invitation.
  2. Open Physician CRM V2 and choose Update when the approved beta is available. Confirm the version and build shown in TestFlight. Do not uninstall first.
  3. If Update is absent, check the existing invitation and build availability with support. Do not use an unrelated invitation or reinstall as a workaround.

Use the isolated prototype account

When a beta containing the isolated-scope option is available, launch on an unlocked iPhone, open Mock account controls and choose Use Prototype Sample · Isolated scope. These controls are available even when the original scope is blocked. This opens a separate synthetic account database; it does not repair, reset or replace the legacy database or key. Mock account selection is not authentication.

A short test with fictional records

  1. Open Offices, choose Cedar Sample Office and start a visit.
  2. Enter a fictional recap and check that the draft reports a durable local save.
  3. Background and unlock the app; confirm the draft remains.
  4. Complete the visit and check Saved on this phone. A waiting-to-sync message is expected because there is no CRM backend or server acknowledgment.
  5. Force-quit and relaunch. After each cold start, open Mock account controls and reselect Use Prototype Sample · Isolated scope before checking the saved records; the app initially selects the original mock scope. Restart the iPhone and repeat that selection and check. Report a missing record in the correct scope; do not reset storage.

If storage is blocked

Wait for any local operation to finish. Record the fixed status shown. If available, Inspect preserved storage produces a read-only classification; it does not establish database integrity or perform recovery. Do not copy database files, change keys or remove the old scope. If the isolated prototype scope also fails, stop and contact support.

Share only what helps

TestFlight feedback and support email may include information you voluntarily send. Crop or redact screenshots. Never send patient names, PHI, clinical information, confidential office records, passwords, encryption keys or raw storage files. There is no need to post a public GitHub issue to obtain support.

For data handling and deletion limitations, read the privacy policy. This prototype is not for patient care or emergency communication.
