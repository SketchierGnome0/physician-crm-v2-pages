Support & testing

A small, synthetic prototype for checking native CRM workflows, local durability and optional automatic cloud backup and controlled restore.

Contact support

support@bracketwest.com

Operated by Igor Rodriguez. Include your app version/build, iPhone model, iOS version, the fixed error label if shown, and short steps to reproduce. Use fictional examples only.

Preserve the existing installation. Do not uninstall the app, clear its storage, delete keys or try a recovery/reset procedure. Stop if an operation asks you to do so.

Update through TestFlight

  1. Open Apple’s TestFlight app with the account that accepted your existing invitation.
  2. Open Physician CRM V2 and choose Update when the approved beta is available. Confirm the version and build shown in TestFlight. Do not uninstall first.
  3. If Update is absent, check the existing invitation and build availability with support. Do not use an unrelated invitation or reinstall as a workaround.

Preserve local review scopes

When a beta containing the isolated-scope option is available, launch on an unlocked iPhone, open Mock account controls and choose Use Prototype Sample · Isolated scope. These controls are available even when the original scope is blocked. This opens a separate synthetic account database; it does not repair, reset or replace the legacy database or key. Mock account selection is not authentication. Keep Prototype Sample for retained local review; do not bind or upload its existing records to disposable cloud credentials. Avoid the previously problematic Alex scope.

A short Quick Visit test

  1. Update in place, select the intended synthetic workspace and confirm its existing visits and drafts remain.
  2. Tap Record Visit, choose an office, enter a short fictional note and tap Save. A follow-up is optional.
  3. Check the local save confirmation. If automatic backup is enabled for the verified account and workspace, wait for a separate server-confirmed backup status.
  4. Repeat offline, then reopen the app after connectivity returns. Confirm the locally saved visit remains and any eligible backup resumes without a duplicate.
  5. After a cold start, reselect the intended workspace before checking its records; the app may initially select Alex. Report a missing record in the correct scope; do not reset storage.

Controlled cloud acceptance

After the in-place update, preserve Blair and Casey’s existing synthetic visits, completed outcomes, drafts, receipts and account bindings through the V3-to-V4 migration. Do not clear or rebind either workspace to prepare a test. Avoid the previously problematic Alex scope. Never uninstall, wipe, reset or rotate keys.

CRM records are stored locally in encrypted storage. Cloud login and backup use Supabase. In the Automatic Backup + Quick Visit TestFlight update, automatic backup is optional and Off initially. Once you explicitly enable it for a verified, bound account, eligible visits and completed outcomes finalized while On are queued for backup while the app is active and connected. iOS suspension or termination can pause uploads. Earlier work and work finalized while Off require separate manual review and consent. Drafts and unfinished outcome notes remain local. There is no automatic synchronization, team-wide sharing or guaranteed immediate disaster recovery. This synthetic beta is not approved for real patient information. In Settings & support, open Cloud backup & restore, verify the account and workspace, then choose whether to enable automatic backup. Confirm server acknowledgments, interrupted/restarted retries without duplicate records, restore preview without changes, additive restore, conflict preservation and wrong-account rejection.

After every cold start, reselect the intended test scope before continuing; the app initially selects Alex. Verify native session persistence, background protection, keyboard reachability, light/dark themes and accessibility. Record exact errors and counts. Synthetic cloud fixtures are cleaned up only after acceptance testing, with explicit count verification.

If storage is blocked

Wait for any local operation to finish. Record the fixed status shown. If available, Inspect preserved storage produces a read-only classification; it does not establish database integrity or perform recovery. Do not copy database files, change keys or remove the old scope. If the isolated prototype scope also fails, stop and contact support.

Share only what helps

TestFlight feedback and support email may include information you voluntarily send. Crop or redact screenshots. Never send patient names, PHI, clinical information, confidential office records, passwords, encryption keys or raw storage files. There is no need to post a public GitHub issue to obtain support.

For data handling and deletion limitations, read the privacy policy. This prototype is not for patient care or emergency communication.
