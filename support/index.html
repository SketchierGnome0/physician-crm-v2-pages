Update through TestFlight

  1. Open Apple’s TestFlight app with the account that accepted your existing invitation.
  2. Open Physician CRM V2 and choose Update when the approved beta is available. Confirm the version and build shown in TestFlight. Do not uninstall first.
  3. If Update is absent, check the existing invitation and build availability with support. Do not use an unrelated invitation or reinstall as a workaround.

A short Quick Visit test

  1. In your personal CRM, tap Record Visit, choose a sample office, enter a short fictional note and Save. Add a follow-up only if needed. Aim for 15–30 seconds.
  2. Confirm the saved status distinguishes synced, saved locally and waiting, or needs attention. No backup selection or extra consent should interrupt the visit.
  3. Quit and reopen; confirm the visit and drafts remain and the personal CRM returns without another password or consent.
  4. During genuine loading delays, check stable skeleton placeholders. Check Reduced Motion, VoiceOver, light/dark appearance and background concealment. Errors must remain visible.

Offline work and reconnection

After successful online verification, an existing personal workspace supports up to 72 hours of local offline work. Disconnect, save a fictional visit, and restart the app. Local records and queued work must remain available within the lease, including after the ordinary one-hour JWT expires. Reconnect and reopen; online verification should renew local access quietly and eligible work should receive a matching cloud receipt without duplicates. Never change the owner’s device clock; the 72-hour boundary can be checked with isolated test instrumentation.

Offline use never renews the deadline. After expiry, sign in or reconnect for authorization; locked records must remain intact. Remote revocation cannot be checked offline. Detected rejection, sign-out and an account change must conceal and isolate the personal workspace. Do not use another person’s credentials or enroll a new user.

Eligible finalized visits and completed outcomes queue automatically only after the one-time consent. Historical work and work finalized while cloud saving is off are excluded. Drafts and unfinished outcome notes remain on this phone. Uploads may pause during iOS suspension; foreground connectivity and fresh authorization resume them. Complete replacement-device recovery and multi-device synchronization are not implemented. Keep the existing synthetic account and backups; cleanup is not part of this evaluation.

Share only what helps

Never send patient names, PHI, clinical information, confidential office records, passwords, encryption keys or raw storage files. There is no need to post a public GitHub issue to obtain support.
