Privacy policy

Effective October 29, 2026 · U1B account-first synthetic beta

Synthetic testing only. Do not enter real patient information, PHI, clinical details or confidential production data. Physician CRM V2 is not a patient-care or clinical application. No HIPAA-compliance claim is made for this beta.

Who operates this beta

Physician CRM V2 is individually developed and operated by Igor Rodriguez. For support or privacy questions, contact support@bracketwest.com. This policy covers the current synthetic iPhone beta and this public privacy/support website; it does not describe unimplemented production features.

Information stored in the app

The beta stores fictional offices, visit recaps, dates, materials, follow-up actions, completed outcomes and unfinished drafts. Local identifiers, save receipts, history and queued operations support durable saves. A verified cloud account opens its own separate encrypted personal workspace. Returning sessions reopen that workspace after access checks. No mock-account choice or manual ownership binding is required in normal onboarding.

Existing Blair, Casey, Alex and Prototype testing workspaces remain separate. Their records are never silently transferred, associated with the personal account or uploaded. Synthetic testing controls are available separately under Testing & support. A local save, queued upload and matching server acknowledgment are distinct states.

Encryption and local safeguards

The reviewed beta requires SQLCipher for the CRM database and uses Expo SecureStore to hold database key material in the iOS Keychain, configured for device-only access while unlocked. The app also configures iOS file protection and backup exclusion for its CRM storage. These safeguards do not make a device or app immune to compromise, and encryption is not a recovery or backup service.

Normal U1B entry requires a verified synthetic cloud account. Previously verified personal workspaces support bounded offline access as described below. Private cloud persistence is for controlled synthetic testing; there is no real-user enrollment, generalized synchronization or configured app-operated analytics, advertising or tracking integration. Apple TestFlight, Supabase, your email service and this website’s host handle the separate information described below.

Synthetic account sign-in

The current beta uses the dedicated Physician CRM V2 Supabase project in the US West region for verified email/password sign-in and private persistence. Public signup is disabled. Accounts are administratively provisioned disposable test identities, not real-user enrollment.

When you sign in, Supabase processes the test account’s email/password and authentication requests and issues session tokens. The app does not persist the password. Session material uses a separate device-only SecureStore service; signing in does not replace or rotate the CRM database key. Supabase and its infrastructure process authentication and API requests, including technical request information, under their service practices. See Supabase’s privacy policy.

One-time consent and automatic saving

After verified sign-in, the personal workspace presents Keep new work safe. Agree and continue gives ongoing consent for eligible visits and completed follow-up outcomes finalized from that point onward. These save locally and automatically queue for the verified private account. Normal visits need no backup button, batch selection or per-visit cloud decision. Earlier records and work saved while cloud saving is off are excluded from automatic consent.

Cloud payloads contain finalized fictional visits, office/catalog references, materials, follow-ups and completed outcome notes, with stable record/retry identifiers, actors and timestamps. Drafts and unfinished outcome notes remain local. Turning Automatically save new work to cloud off pauses uploads and stops automatic eligibility for newly finalized work; it does not erase previously consented or acknowledged records.

Local saves work without connectivity. Queued operations survive restart, but transmit only after successful online authorization. Uploads run while the app is active and connected; iOS suspension or termination may pause them. Only matching server receipts establish cloud persistence. Conflicting versions are preserved. There is no complete multi-device synchronization, team-wide sharing or automatic replacement-device recovery. Do not rely on the beta as a production backup service.

Offline access, sign-out and test preservation

An existing, previously verified personal workspace may be used offline for at most 72 hours from the last successful online identity and authorized data check. Offline use cannot create a new workspace or encryption key and cannot extend the deadline. JWT/API-token lifetimes are unchanged. Fresh online authorization is required before queued work transmits. Remote revocation cannot be detected while disconnected. Clock rollback checks fail closed, but the device clock is not tamperproof across restarts or device compromise.

When the lease expires, access locks and local records are retained. Detected revocation, authorization rejection, account mismatch and sign-out invalidate local access. Signing out removes the local cloud session and protects the workspace; it does not delete encrypted records, drafts, queued operations or hosted backups. Background concealment protects the ordinary screen. Another account opens a separate workspace.

Retain the existing synthetic account, cloud test records and legacy workspaces during this evaluation. Cleanup requires separate authorization; this release performs none. Server-confirmed records remain available only while the account is authorized and the records are retained. Existing controlled restore previews preserve local records and unfinished work, but cannot automatically reconstruct a replacement device.

We use beta information made available to us to troubleshoot problems and improve the prototype, subject to Apple’s terms. Apple handles its own collection and retention. See Apple’s TestFlight & Privacy notice for details and options. Do not include patient or confidential information in feedback.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until affected by app or operating-system storage actions. The beta does not offer a complete account-erasure or legacy-storage-reset control. Closing the app, signing out of a mock account or switching scopes does not erase stored records. Uninstalling can remove local database files while iOS Keychain entries can persist; reinstalling is not a verified complete-deletion or recovery method.

Do not uninstall, clear storage or delete keys to troubleshoot the current beta. Existing legacy storage is being preserved, and this beta does not enable its recovery or reset. Support cannot remotely inspect, restore or erase the phone’s local database. Optional server-confirmed synthetic backups have the separate authorization and test-cleanup limits described above. Local-only work is not remotely backed up; device loss, erasure or key loss may make it inaccessible.

Support correspondence and beta troubleshooting records may be retained while needed to address the request, investigate problems, maintain security or meet legal obligations. No fixed deletion deadline is promised. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you provided; we will review what we can address, including technical and legal limits. Apple and GitHub control retention in their own services; deleting local app data does not delete messages or TestFlight records.
