Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · Current synthetic beta

Synthetic testing only. Do not enter real patient information, PHI, clinical details or confidential production data. Physician CRM V2 is not a patient-care or clinical application. No HIPAA-compliance claim is made for this beta.

Who operates this beta

Physician CRM V2 is individually developed and operated by Igor Rodriguez. For support or privacy questions, contact support@bracketwest.com. This policy covers the current synthetic iPhone beta and this public privacy/support website; it does not describe unimplemented production features.

Information stored in the app

The beta stores information you enter in fictional CRM workflows: selected offices, visit recaps, dates and time zones, materials, follow-up actions and due dates, and unfinished drafts. Local identifiers, account scope, save receipts, history and queued operation records support reliable saves and reopening.

These records are stored in account-scoped local databases. Mock account selection is a testing control, not production authentication. Switching a mock account retains that scope’s drafts and records; it does not delete them. A “Waiting to sync” state does not mean anything has been uploaded or acknowledged by a server.

Encryption and local safeguards

The reviewed beta requires SQLCipher for the CRM database and uses Expo SecureStore to hold database key material in the iOS Keychain, configured for device-only access while unlocked. The app also configures iOS file protection and backup exclusion for its CRM storage. These safeguards do not make a device or app immune to compromise, and encryption is not a recovery or backup service.

The current beta has no production identity service, CRM backend or cloud synchronization, and no configured app-operated analytics, advertising or tracking integration. This statement does not exclude the separate information handled by Apple TestFlight, your email service or this website’s host.

Apple TestFlight

Apple automatically collects beta crash logs and usage information, and makes information available to the application provider. This can include invitation and installation status, app and device versions, sessions, crashes, and a tester’s name or email depending on how they joined.

Feedback you choose to submit through TestFlight can include comments, screenshots and diagnostic context. We use beta information made available to us to troubleshoot problems and improve the prototype, subject to Apple’s terms. Apple handles its own collection and retention. See Apple’s TestFlight & Privacy notice for details and options. Do not include patient or confidential information in feedback.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive the address, message and any device details, screenshots or attachments you choose to send. Support correspondence is handled through email services and may remain in message histories and service backups. We use it to respond, investigate issues, maintain security and address applicable legal obligations. Send only what is necessary; do not send database files, encryption keys, credentials, patient information or unredacted sensitive screenshots.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics or tracking scripts, advertising, contact forms, embedded third-party content or externally loaded fonts. The site does not set application cookies or use browser storage. GitHub and its infrastructure process requests to deliver the site and may handle technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Email links open your chosen email service.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until affected by app or operating-system storage actions. The beta does not offer a complete account-erasure or legacy-storage-reset control. Closing the app, signing out of a mock account or switching scopes does not erase stored records. Uninstalling can remove local database files while iOS Keychain entries can persist; reinstalling is not a verified complete-deletion or recovery method.

Do not uninstall, clear storage or delete keys to troubleshoot the current beta. Existing legacy storage is being preserved, and this beta does not enable its recovery or reset. Without a CRM backend or cloud copy, support cannot remotely inspect, restore or erase the local database. Device loss, erasure or key loss may make local records inaccessible.

Support correspondence and beta troubleshooting records may be retained while needed to address the request, investigate problems, maintain security or meet legal obligations. No fixed deletion deadline is promised. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you provided; we will review what we can address, including technical and legal limits. Apple and GitHub control retention in their own services; deleting local app data does not delete messages or TestFlight records.

Questions and changes

For a privacy question, use support@bracketwest.com. Do not include sensitive data just to establish your request. Material changes to this beta’s data handling will require an updated policy with a revised effective date.
