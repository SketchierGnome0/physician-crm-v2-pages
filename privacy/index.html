Privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026 · Automatic Backup + Quick Visit synthetic beta

Synthetic testing only. Do not enter real patient information, PHI, clinical details or confidential production data. Physician CRM V2 is not a patient-care or clinical application. No HIPAA-compliance claim is made for this beta.

Who operates this beta

Physician CRM V2 is individually developed and operated by Igor Rodriguez. For support or privacy questions, contact support@bracketwest.com. This policy covers the current synthetic iPhone beta and this public privacy/support website; it does not describe unimplemented production features.

Information stored in the app

The beta stores information you enter in fictional CRM workflows: selected offices, visit recaps, dates and time zones, materials, follow-up actions and due dates, and unfinished drafts. Local identifiers, account scope, save receipts, history and queued operation records support reliable saves and reopening.

These records are stored in account-scoped local databases. Mock account selection is a testing control, not production authentication. Switching a mock account retains that scope’s drafts and records; it does not delete them. A local save, upload staging and server-confirmed backup are separate states. Signing in or selecting a mock account does not upload CRM records.

Encryption and local safeguards

The reviewed beta requires SQLCipher for the CRM database and uses Expo SecureStore to hold database key material in the iOS Keychain, configured for device-only access while unlocked. The app also configures iOS file protection and backup exclusion for its CRM storage. These safeguards do not make a device or app immune to compromise, and encryption is not a recovery or backup service.

Local CRM use remains available without cloud sign-in. The current beta adds optional authentication and private cloud backup for controlled synthetic testing; there is no real-user enrollment, generalized synchronization or configured app-operated analytics, advertising or tracking integration. Apple TestFlight, Supabase, your email service and this website’s host handle the separate information described below.

Optional synthetic cloud testing

The current beta uses the dedicated Physician CRM V2 Supabase project in the US West region for optional email/password sign-in and private backup/restore. Public signup is disabled. Accounts are administratively provisioned disposable test identities, not real-user enrollment.

When you sign in, Supabase processes the test account’s email/password and authentication requests and issues session tokens. The app does not persist the password. Session material uses a separate device-only SecureStore service; signing in does not replace or rotate the CRM database key. Supabase and its infrastructure process authentication and API requests, including technical request information, under their service practices. See Supabase’s privacy policy.

Binding, consent and backup contents

Signing in uploads no CRM records. You must explicitly bind the selected synthetic workspace to its verified cloud account. In the Automatic Backup + Quick Visit TestFlight update, automatic backup is optional and Off initially. Enabling it is a separate, one-time choice that gives ongoing consent to back up eligible work finalized while it is On. Previously finalized work, and work finalized while it is Off, still requires a separate manual review and consent before upload. The permanent workspace binding is separate from the session. Check the account and workspace before enabling or consenting; do not bind a workspace to disposable credentials without reviewing its existing work.

Consented backups contain finalized fictional visits, office/catalog references, materials, follow-up actions and completed outcome notes, with original record/retry identifiers, historical actors and timestamps. Unfinished visit drafts and unfinished outcome notes stay local. Eligible records upload while the app is active and connected; iOS suspension or termination can pause uploads until the app reopens. A local save or queued upload is not a confirmed cloud backup. Backups are scoped to the authenticated owner; there is no team-wide record sharing or automatic synchronization. Retries use stable identities and do not silently replace conflicting versions. Cloud backup is not guaranteed immediate disaster recovery or a production backup service.

Cloud sign-out and test cleanup

Turning automatic backup Off stops newly finalized work from receiving automatic upload consent; it does not revoke consent already used for work finalized while it was On. Cloud sign-out removes the app’s cloud session on that device. It does not erase the encrypted local CRM database, permanent binding, staged work or hosted backups. Session revocation and access-token expiry are distinct. The ordinary backup client cannot delete existing cloud rows. After controlled acceptance testing, the operator revokes disposable sessions, removes only authorized synthetic account/record fixtures and verifies cleanup. No real-user or production-data retention service is offered.

A server-confirmed record is available for authorized read/restore only while its account remains authorized and the record is retained. Records removed during test cleanup are no longer restorable. Restore previews are validated and additions preserve existing local records and unfinished work. Do not rely on this synthetic beta for production records.

Apple TestFlight

Apple automatically collects beta crash logs and usage information, and makes information available to the application provider. This can include invitation and installation status, app and device versions, sessions, crashes, and a tester’s name or email depending on how they joined.

Feedback you choose to submit through TestFlight can include comments, screenshots and diagnostic context. We use beta information made available to us to troubleshoot problems and improve the prototype, subject to Apple’s terms. Apple handles its own collection and retention. See Apple’s TestFlight & Privacy notice for details and options. Do not include patient or confidential information in feedback.

Support correspondence

If you email us, we receive the address, message and any device details, screenshots or attachments you choose to send. Support correspondence is handled through email services and may remain in message histories and service backups. We use it to respond, investigate issues, maintain security and address applicable legal obligations. Send only what is necessary; do not send database files, encryption keys, credentials, patient information or unredacted sensitive screenshots.

This website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics or tracking scripts, advertising, contact forms, embedded third-party content or externally loaded fonts. The site does not set application cookies or use browser storage. GitHub and its infrastructure process requests to deliver the site and may handle technical information such as IP addresses and request logs under GitHub’s Privacy Statement. Email links open your chosen email service.

Retention, deletion and your choices

Local records remain until affected by app or operating-system storage actions. The beta does not offer a complete account-erasure or legacy-storage-reset control. Closing the app, signing out of a mock account or switching scopes does not erase stored records. Uninstalling can remove local database files while iOS Keychain entries can persist; reinstalling is not a verified complete-deletion or recovery method.

Do not uninstall, clear storage or delete keys to troubleshoot the current beta. Existing legacy storage is being preserved, and this beta does not enable its recovery or reset. Support cannot remotely inspect, restore or erase the phone’s local database. Optional server-confirmed synthetic backups have the separate authorization and test-cleanup limits described above. Local-only work is not remotely backed up; device loss, erasure or key loss may make it inaccessible.

Support correspondence and beta troubleshooting records may be retained while needed to address the request, investigate problems, maintain security or meet legal obligations. No fixed deletion deadline is promised. Contact us to ask about access, correction or deletion of information you provided; we will review what we can address, including technical and legal limits. Apple and GitHub control retention in their own services; deleting local app data does not delete messages or TestFlight records.

Questions and changes

For a privacy question, use support@bracketwest.com. Do not include sensitive data just to establish your request. Material changes to this beta’s data handling will require an updated policy with a revised effective date.
